## What we collect, and why

When you create an account we ask for:

| What | Why | Shown publicly? |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | To confirm you are reachable and to reset your password | No |
| Password | To sign you in. We store a bcrypt hash, never the password itself | No |
| Real name | So that one person holds one account | Only if you choose to show it |
| Display name | The name most people will see | Yes, unless you choose anonymous |
| Date of birth | To check you are old enough to join. We check once, at signup | No |
| Home city and county | To put your posts and your vote in the right community | Yes, as a community, not as an address |
| Gender | Aggregate public reporting only | Only as part of a total |
| Political party | Aggregate public reporting only | Only as part of a total |

Gender and political party never change what you see. The platform does not
profile you, does not infer your politics from your behaviour, and does not
personalize anything except through settings you can see and change yourself.

## What we never do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not accept advertising.
- We do not use your data to train a model without saying so.
- We do not weight your vote by how verified you are, or by anything else.
  Every vote counts the same.

## What is public

Your posts, solutions, comments, amendments and workshop votes are public, with
your name shown according to your display setting. **Your ballot votes are
not.** A ballot vote is shown to exactly one person — you — on the ballot page
and in your own data export. Administrators see counts only.

## Your rights

- **Export.** You can download everything the platform holds about you, at any
  time, as a JSON file.
- **Deletion.** You can delete your account at any time. We permanently erase
  your name, email, password, date of birth, gender and political party. We
  keep your home city and county, because the civic record has to stay in the
  right community, and neither identifies you on their own. Everything you
  wrote stays, attributed to "Former Community Member".
- **Content fingerprints stay.** Every post, solution and published document
  has a cryptographic fingerprint — a hash. Those are public proofs that the
  record was not altered, not personal data, and they are never deleted. We
  tell you this at signup.

## Cookies

One cookie: the refresh token that keeps you signed in. It is `httpOnly` and
`SameSite=Strict`, which means no other website can read it. We use no
analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies.
