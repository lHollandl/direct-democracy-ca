## What this platform is

Direct Democracy Cali is a place for California residents to document problems
in their community, propose and improve solutions together, vote on them, and
send the result to the people who represent them.

It is **not** a government system. It has no authority. Its output is a
document and the pressure of the people who choose to send it.

## Who can join

Anyone who is at least the minimum age shown on the public settings page (17
today) and who lives in California. We ask for your city and county and we
take your word for it: **residency is self-declared and we cannot verify it.**
Every published result says so.

## What you agree to

- One account per person, under your real name, whatever name you display.
- You will not post anything unlawful, or anything that identifies a private
  person without their consent.
- Every post must include at least one proposed solution. This platform is not
  a place to complain without proposing.

## What happens to what you write

**Solutions belong to the community once posted.** Your authorship is recorded
and displayed, but you do not keep control: anyone in the community may propose
an amendment, and if enough people support it, the text changes. That is the
point of the workshop.

Posts cannot be edited or deleted in this build. A post is fingerprinted when
it is created and that fingerprint is permanent.

## AI on this platform

AI sorts, suggests and recommends. **It never decides.** It never creates a
category, never casts or weights a vote, never advances anything through a
threshold. Every action it takes is labelled where you see it and recorded in a
public log you can read at `/ai/actions`. You can correct anything it labels,
and your correction is recorded too.

## Sending results to representatives

When you press "Send to my representatives", your own email program opens with
a message already written. **You send it, from your own address.** The platform
sends nothing and records nothing about whether you pressed send.

In this demo build every address in the officials directory is a test address
belonging to the director, not a real official.

## Moderation

There is none in this build. That is a known gap, recorded publicly, and it
must be closed before the platform opens beyond a handful of test accounts.

## No warranty

This is an unfinished demo running on one machine. It may lose data, be taken
offline, or be rebuilt from scratch. Do not rely on it.
